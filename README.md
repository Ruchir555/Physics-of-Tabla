# Physics of Tabla

An open research and educational project exploring the **physics of tabla sound** and the **mathematics of Hindustani rhythm**.

Topics include vibrating membranes, Bessel modes, the syahi, Fourier analysis, musical intervals, taal, layakari, cyclic groups, and tihai construction.

## Tutorials

- **[Tabla acoustics](tutorials/tabla-acoustics/README.md):** harmonic spectra, membrane vibrations, syahi loading and bol acoustics.
- **[Mathematics of rhythm](tutorials/mathematics-of-rhythm/README.md):** Dirac combs, taal cycles, Fourier analysis, cyclic symmetry and tihais.

This repository is an early-stage work in progress. The tutorials and models are educational; numerical examples are not presented as experimentally validated descriptions of a specific instrument. References and derivations will undergo further review.

**Author:** Ruchir Tullu

## Roadmap

Expand and verify the tutorials; develop reproducible simulations and audio experiments; assemble a rigorous book-length treatment.
